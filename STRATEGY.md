# FENORA — COMPREHENSIVE MARKETING STRATEGY & CONTENT BLUEPRINT

> **"One job. One place. From survey to signed off."**  
> An operating system built for the physical reality of the window, door, glazing, and construction trade.

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Construction businesses are already running on software. They just have:
- WhatsApp (47 unread voice notes)
- Excel (a quoting sheet made in 2018 with 80 broken macros)
- Google Drive & Dropbox (400 folders named `FINAL_USE_THIS_ONE`)
- Email inboxes (quotes buried under supplier spam)
- Paper notebooks under the passenger seat of the Transit van
- Scribbles on plasterboard offcuts
- And Dave's laptop

**The enemy is not another SaaS vendor. The enemy is "the way you've always done it."**

Fenora does not market itself as "CRM for builders" or a "game-changing SaaS tool". Fenora is positioned as the **operating system that understands the entire physical lifecycle of a job**: 2D parametric drawings, structural deductions, live quoting, mobile site surveys, variation management, supplier procurement, delivery dispatch, and instant milestone invoicing.

## PART B: BRAND VOICE & FOREMAN RULES

### 1. Tone & Personality
- **Straight-Talking & Construction-Native:** Sounds like a veteran site foreman with 20 years on the tools.
- **Dry & Cynical:** Humour grounded in the absurd reality of British construction sites, weather, and customer psychology.
- **Razor-Sharp on Detail:** Knows the difference between a stub cill and an extension sub-cill; understands BS 8213-4 and Part F ventilation.
- **Commercial & Margin-Focused:** Speaks directly to cash flow, labour stand-downs, and profitability.

### 2. The 9 Golden Foreman Rules
1. **Pass the Van Test:** Would an actual 35-year-old window fitter scrolling their phone in a Transit van at 6:30 PM relate to this? If not, rewrite it.
2. **Never Sound Like a Marketing Agency:** No corporate cheerleading. No faux enthusiasm.
3. **Expose the Recognisable Construction Lie First:** Start with the absurdity (the famous last words, the napkin sketch), then land the punchline.
4. **Concrete Site Consequences:** Never talk about "operational efficiency"; talk about an aluminium frame standing on the driveway that is 35mm too wide.
5. **No Generic Construction Puns:** Ban "Nailed it", "Groundbreaking", "Building success", and "Raising the roof".
6. **The Tradesperson is Never the Villain:** The villain is the fragmented software stack and "the way it's always been done".
7. **AI Without the Bullshit:** Never claim AI is "revolutionising construction". Demonstrate how it extracts specs from messy tender PDFs.
8. **Foreman Humour Style:** Brutal, funny, self-deprecating, and laser-accurate:
   - *"I’d explain it to you, but I left my crayons at home."*
   - *"You’ve got two brain cells and they’re both fighting for third place."*
   - *"If common sense was petrol, you couldn’t drive a piss-ant scooter across a fucking matchstick."*
   - *"You’re not completely useless. You can always serve as a bad example."*
   - *"I’ve seen wet cement with more structural integrity than you."*
   - *"You couldn’t pour piss out of a boot if the instructions were written on the heel."*
   - *"You’ve got the work ethic of a Sunday afternoon."*
   - *"I’ve met fucking idiots before, but you’ve got tenure."*
   - *"You’re living proof that evolution sometimes takes a lunch break."*
9. **Never Invent Customer Results:** Label hypothetical examples as illustrative models.

### 3. Voice Comparison (Say This vs Never Say That)
| Say This (Fenora Voice) | Never Say That (Generic SaaS Cringe) |
|---|---|
| *"A 10-person window company doesn't need 14 different software subscriptions."* | *"Revolutionise your contracting workflow with our synergistic platform!"* |

- **70% Peers (Fitters, Joiners, Surveyors, Builders):** Relatable site humour, memes, famous last words, failure breakdowns. Builds audience volume, algorithmic momentum, and brand affinity.
- **25% Buyers (MDs, Owners, Ops Directors, Commercial Managers):** Landed job costing, regulation updates (Part L/F), variation governance, cash flow acceleration. Drives inbound DMs and demo investigations.
- **5% Product (Direct Solution Walkthrough):** 2D parametric configurator demos, DXF export, mobile survey walkthroughs. Drives high-intent trial conversions.

### 2. The 8 Content Pillars
1. **Trade Pain & Relatability:** The absurd reality of British construction sites.
2. **The Chaos of Running a Trade Business:** What happens between the quote and the invoice.
3. **Fenora Product Education:** Problem → Site Chaos → Fenora Solution.
4. **Before / After Workflows:** Visual contrasts between 7 fragmented tools and one unified record.
5. **Technical & Regulatory Education:** Part F, Part L, bay window triangulation, cill projection physics.
6. **Build In Public:** Developing a construction-native operating system with trade input.
7. **Customer & Industry Realities:** True site war stories, customer quirks, and contractor lessons.
8. **AI Without the Bullshit:** Pragmatic administrative automation that removes data-entry misery.

### 3. Platform Distribution Roles
- **Instagram (@fenora.pro):** Top-of-funnel reach via Reels (5–30s) and Carousels (6 slides). Visual, fast-paced, irreverent. Fitters scroll at 08:15 and 18:30.
- **Facebook (@fenora.pro):** Community engagement, debate starters, trade polls, and war story threads in contractor groups. Highly conversational.
- **LinkedIn (@fenora.pro):** Executive and commercial authority. Long-form posts targeting owners and managing directors on margins, scaling bottlenecks, and operational debt.
