The 20-minute daily routine

The half of the strategy that isn't your own posts. Same time every day. Never pitch.

  1. 0–4 min — Search. On Instagram and TikTok: window fitter, double glazing fail, cowboy builder caught, trades life, plasterer, joiner, survey fail, conservation area, kitchen extension fail, build in public. Plus every trades account near you.
  2. 4–10 min — Watch properly. 20–30 seconds each. Skip in under 2 seconds and your whole feed suffers.
  3. 10–18 min — Eight comments. On the specific thing in that video, not a lazy fire emoji. Use the templates. Reply to every reply.
  4. 18–20 min — Log it. Tick the counter. That is the whole discipline.
